Lightweight skin lesion classification and acne severity grading with uncertainty-aware referral

Research prototype, not a medical device. Predictions are produced by a model trained on public research datasets and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Images are processed entirely in your browser and are never uploaded.

    How it works

    1. Pre-processing. Resize shorter side to 224 px, centre crop 224×224, ImageNet normalisation.
    2. Frozen backbone. ImageNet-pretrained PP-LCNet-1.0 produces a 512×7×7 feature map.
    3. CBAM attention. Channel and spatial attention re-weight the features before global average pooling.
    4. Two task heads. A 7-class lesion head (trained on HAM10000) and a 4-grade acne head (trained on ACNE04) share the pooled features.
    5. Monte Carlo dropout. 30 stochastic passes (dropout 0.3) give the mean probabilities and the normalised predictive entropy. Cases with entropy above 0.4 are flagged for specialist review.
    6. Explanation. The class activation map weights the attention-refined feature map by the predicted class's head weights.
    7. Reporting. A schema-validated JSON payload is turned into text; any text that differs from the payload is rejected.

    Data are split by lesion (HAM10000) or patient (PAD-UFES-20) and de-duplicated with SHA-256 hashes, so images of one lesion never appear in both training and test sets.
